# 13.04 - Remove the `setting` and `depends` directives from the language

> File: `.claude/plans/13.04-remove-setting-and-depends.md`
> The owner decided these are not needed. This is a deliberate revert of part of
> milestone 10, not a cleanup — it must be recorded as one.

## Objective

Take `setting` and `depends` out of the parser, the checker, the manifest, and
the editor, leaving `export` as the only build directive and
`ManifestContribution` as a channel with one shape.

## Scope

- **In:** The directive syntax, their AST nodes, their checks, their manifest
  elements, their LSP completion and hover, and their tests.
- **Out:** `export`, which stays exactly as it is. The contribution channel
  itself stays too — 13.3 and 13.5 both use it.

## Steps

- [ ] Remove `setting` and `depends` from the parser and from
      `parser/directive-nodes.ts`, keeping `export` parsing untouched.
- [ ] Remove `SettingContribution` and `IncludeContribution` from
      `project/manifest.ts`, along with `settingElements`, `includeElements`,
      and the `*` setting prefix.
- [ ] Remove `project-duplicate-setting` and every check that exists only for
      these two directives. Keep `project-duplicate-export`.
- [ ] Remove them from LSP completion, hover, and the VS Code grammar, so the
      words stop being highlighted as modifiers.
- [ ] Confirm both words are plain identifiers again: `local setting = 1` and a
      function named `depends` called with parentheses both compile.
- [ ] Delete the tests that covered them rather than skipping them, and note in
      [ADR-007](../docs/adr/007-build-directives.md) that this part is
      superseded by [ADR-008](../docs/adr/008-generated-manifest-standard.md).
- [ ] Check `examples/`, the template, and the scaffold for any use, and remove
      it there too.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `setting MAX_PLAYERS = 32` no longer parses as a directive; it is read as
      ordinary source and fails or binds like any other identifier expression.
- [ ] No `<setting>` and no `<include>` element can be produced by any input.
- [ ] `export` behaves exactly as it did: the same emitted Lua, the same
      diagnostics, the same duplicate check.
- [ ] Adding a future directive still touches only the parser, the checker, the
      AST, and `project/manifest.ts` — the milestone 10 property survives with
      one directive in the channel.
- [ ] `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm build`, and `pnpm test` pass with no skipped test.
